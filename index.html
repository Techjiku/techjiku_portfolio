About Techjiku

Techjiku is a student deep-tech startup from Chaudhary Devi Lal University, Sirsa, building direct-to-consumer and custom hardware products across AI, autonomous robotics, IoT, and rapid fabrication.

We turn local problems into deployable products by moving from CAD prototype to field testing and iteration, with a focus on practical automation for agriculture and industrial workflows.

Techjiku Vision & Core Product Philosophy

  • Product-First Approach

    We bridge the gap between academic research and commercial technology. Every system is built to become a scalable, deployable product.

  • Structured Engineering

    Our agile cycle: Problem → CAD Prototype → Field Testing → Iteration → D2C / B2B Commercial Product.

  • Lab to Field Deployment

    Moving robotics out of simulation environments and deploying real-world hardware directly into agricultural and industrial floors.

Journey & Stack

Startup Journey

  1. Problem Discovery

    Phase 1

    Identifying labor-heavy seed drying and automation gaps in indoor agricultural workflows.

  2. Prototype Development

    Phase 2

    Building CAD models, control systems, and autonomous motion hardware for the first working robot.

  3. Product Validation

    Phase 3

    Iterating through field testing to turn prototype hardware into deployable D2C and B2B products.
